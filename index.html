ABOUT US

Our Story

Welcome to Prince Coffee Shop, a cozy place created for coffee lovers and people who appreciate life's simple pleasures.

We believe every cup of coffee brings people together. Our goal is to offer delicious drinks, friendly service, and a relaxing atmosphere where everyone feels welcome.

Alongside our coffee, enjoy our local favorites: Malunggay Bread with Cheese, Rice Puto with Cheese and Puto Maya served with chocolate & Mango.
